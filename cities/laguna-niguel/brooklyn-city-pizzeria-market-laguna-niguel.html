Real New York Pizza in Laguna Niguel

I went looking for a proper New York slice in Laguna Niguel and found it at Brooklyn City Pizzeria & Market, a husband-and-wife spot on Crown Valley Parkway where the pies are 18 inches, the dough is cold-rested for three days or more, and the pepperoni cups up the way it should.

Overview

Brooklyn City Pizzeria & Market is run by Gus and Margarita Floris, a husband-and-wife team, and Gus is a Brooklyn native who cooks like it. The room works as both a pizzeria and a small market, so you can sit down for a slice or grab a pie to take home. The dough is the story here: it is cold-rested for three days or more before it ever hits the oven, which gives the crust that chew and slight tang you only get when somebody is not rushing the process.

With a 4.7-star rating across more than 950 Yelp reviews, this is one of the best-reviewed pizza spots in Laguna Niguel, and it earns it. This is not a place trying to reinvent pizza. It is a place doing the classic New York pie right, one 18-inch round at a time.

Where to Find It

Brooklyn City Pizzeria & Market sits at 30012 Crown Valley Parkway, Suite L, in Laguna Niguel. It is right on Crown Valley, easy to get to from anywhere in town, with plaza parking out front. Call ahead at (949) 363-7777 if you want a whole pie ready when you arrive.

Hours

  • Monday: Closed
  • Tuesday – Thursday: 11 AM – 8 PM
  • Friday – Saturday: 11 AM – 9 PM
  • Sunday: 11 AM – 8 PM

What to Order

Start with the pepperoni pie. The pepperoni cups up into little crisp-edged bowls as it cooks, and on a crust that has had three days to develop, it is the slice to judge the place by. The 18-inch pies are the move if you are feeding a group, and a slice or two works just as well if you are stopping in solo. Since it is also a market, it is worth a look around after you eat.

Why I Like It

I like that nothing here feels fussy. Gus and Margarita built a neighborhood pizzeria around a Brooklyn idea of what pizza should be: a big pie, a crust with real flavor, and a room you can walk into without a plan. The three-day dough is not a gimmick, you can taste it in the chew, and the pepperoni cups are exactly the detail that tells you they care about the small stuff.

Best Time to Go

Weekday lunch is the easy play: walk in around 11, grab a slice, and you are out without a wait. Friday and Saturday nights are the busiest, and the room stays open until 9, so plan on a little more of a crowd. One thing to know before you go: the pizzeria is closed on Mondays.

Final Thoughts

If you want New York pizza without leaving Laguna Niguel, this is the stop. Order the pepperoni, take your time with the crust, and save room to browse the market side before you head out.
