El Zarape in Cypress, CA: Family-Owned Mexican Food and Homemade Flavor

For diners looking for a neighborhood Mexican restaurant in Cypress, El Zarape brings a longstanding family-owned presence to Ball Road. Established in 1990, this casual strip-mall spot is known for its welcoming, unpretentious character, homemade Mexican dishes, and margaritas. Its decades in the community make it a place to consider for a relaxed meal, a familiar gathering, or a casual night out close to home.
