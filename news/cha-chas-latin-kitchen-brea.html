Cha Cha's Latin Kitchen

Crab and shrimp enchiladas, live music, and a wood-fired oven in downtown Brea.

Overview

Cha Cha’s Latin Kitchen has been the lively anchor of downtown Brea since 2009, and it still feels like the party. It’s a locally owned Mexican and Latin American spot with a wood-fired oven, live music, an all-day weekday happy hour, and daily deals that give the week a rhythm — Taco Tuesday, Enchilada Wednesday, and a Sunday brunch that runs until 3:00.

The room is warm, loud in a good way, and built for groups. Come with friends and order for the table.

Where to Find It

Cha Cha’s Latin Kitchen
110 W Birch St #7, Brea, CA 92821
Phone: (714) 255-1040

Hours

  • Monday - Thursday: 11:30 AM - 9:30 PM
  • Friday - Saturday: 11:30 AM - 10:30 PM
  • Sunday: 10:30 AM - 9:30 PM

Sunday brunch runs 10:30 AM to 3:00 PM. Note that delivery apps show earlier closing times — those are delivery cutoffs, not the dining room’s hours.

What to Order

The Crab & Shrimp Enchiladas are the go-to — the plate regulars order first and the one Wednesday enchilada night is built around. The Cha Cha’s Fries, crisp potatoes with chipotle aioli, get called the star of the show for a reason. The Slow Roasted Carnitas and the Chicken Flautas round out the order nicely.

Why I Like It

Downtown Brea is better with Cha Cha’s in it. Sixteen years in, it’s still the room where the music is on, the oven is going, and the food comes out built for sharing.

Best Time to Go

Taco Tuesday or Enchilada Wednesday if you want the deals, or Sunday brunch if you want the slower version of the same room. Weekday happy hour runs all day, so an early evening stop works any day.

Final Thoughts

If you’re in downtown Brea and want a fun night out that doesn’t feel fussy, Cha Cha’s is the answer. Get the crab and shrimp enchiladas and stay for the music.
