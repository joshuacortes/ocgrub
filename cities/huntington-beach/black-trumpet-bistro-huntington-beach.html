Restaurant Spotlight · Huntington Beach, CA

The Black Trumpet Bistro

Location 7041 Yorktown Ave #104
Huntington Beach, CA 92648
Phone (714) 842-1122
CALL RESTAURANT ↗

Tapas, Paella, and Candlelight in Seacliff

I did not expect to find one of the best date-night rooms in Orange County tucked into a shopping center on Yorktown Avenue. The Black Trumpet Bistro is that kind of surprise: you walk in off the parking lot and suddenly you are in a dim, candlelit bistro with jazz in the background, a wall of wine, and a menu that jumps from Spain to France to Morocco without ever losing its footing.

The room is small and intimate, and that is the point. There is an open kitchen and a bar you can sit at and watch the cooks work, and the whole place feels like it was built for lingering. This is not a quick bite before a movie. This is the kind of dinner where you order a few small plates, then another, then decide you might as well stay for dessert.

It is also the rare Huntington Beach restaurant that feels grown-up without feeling stiff. You can come in for a celebration, a date, or just a Tuesday night when you want something better than usual, and the room works for all of it.

Overview

The Black Trumpet Bistro was created and is owned by Chef Dino Ferraro, and it has been part of Huntington Beach since 2012. It is the sister restaurant to Capone's Italian Cucina, his other family-owned restaurant in town, which has been going for 27 years. That family history matters here, because the cooking has the confidence of people who have been feeding this city for a long time.

The menu is Mediterranean tapas and small plates with flavors pulled from Italy, France, Spain, Greece, and Morocco, plus full entrees for when you want a proper plate of your own. The restaurant was named to the LA Times Top 101 list for 2026, and on Yelp it holds a 4.5-star average across about 1,500 reviews. I could not pin down a direct Yelp page link to share, so look it up by name if you want to read what diners are saying.

Where to Find It

The Black Trumpet sits at 7041 Yorktown Avenue, Suite 104, in the Seacliff Village shopping center in Huntington Beach. It is easy to miss from the street, so look for the center and head in. There is plenty of parking in the lot. Call (714) 842-1122 for a reservation, which I would recommend on a weekend.

Hours

  • Sunday: 11:30 AM–9:00 PM
  • Monday: 4:00 PM–9:00 PM
  • Tuesday: 11:30 AM–9:00 PM
  • Wednesday–Saturday: 11:30 AM–10:00 PM

What to Order

The dish to build your meal around is the Seafood Paella, the one in the photo: saffron risotto with green peas, Spanish chorizo, mussels, clams, shrimp, and calamari, finished with aioli. It is made to share, and it is the plate that tells you what this kitchen can do. The Paella De Carne is its richer cousin, saffron risotto with a lamb chop, duck breast, and pomegranate sauce.

For tapas, start with the truffle fries and the house special risotto, then add the tuna tartare and the Brussels sprouts with chorizo. The Filet Mignon Kebob comes on a skewer with rice, vegetables, tzatziki, and tabbouleh, and the slow-braised Ella Lamb Shank with red wine fig reduction is the move if you want a full entree. The charcuterie board of imported aged meats and artisan cheeses is a good call for the table, and the house-made sangria is worth ordering by the glass.

Why I Like It

I like the Black Trumpet because it is doing something nobody else in Huntington Beach is really doing. The tapas format means you get to taste five or six things instead of committing to one plate, the wine list is genuinely thoughtful, and the room itself, candlelit, a little jazzy, a little dark, makes an ordinary night feel like an occasion. It is the place I would send someone who thinks Huntington Beach is all burgers and beach bars.

Best Time to Go

Weekday lunch is the quiet move here: the room is calm, the light is good, and you can take your time with the menu. Evenings, especially Friday and Saturday, fill up fast, so book ahead. Note that Mondays are dinner only, opening at 4:00 PM, and the kitchen holds tables for about two hours, so plan for a real, unhurried meal rather than a quick stop.

Final Thoughts

Order the seafood paella, a couple of tapas, and a glass of sangria, and settle in. The Black Trumpet is proof that Huntington Beach has a serious dining scene if you know where to look, and this candlelit little bistro in Seacliff is one of the best places to find it.
