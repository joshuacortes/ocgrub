Sababa Falafel Shop

Falafel fried to order, ribeye hummus, and a 4.8-star Garden Grove favorite.

Overview

Sababa Falafel Shop is the kind of fast-casual spot that makes a whole city proud. Opened in 2020, single location, and already ranked #1 on Yelp Elites’ Garden Grove list and in Yelp’s Top Restaurants in Orange County for a third consecutive year — with a 4.8-star rating across more than 2,000 reviews. The falafel is fried to order, the pitas are stuffed properly, and the hummus is the real thing.

It’s a small shop with a big reputation, and the line at lunch tells you everything.

Where to Find It

Sababa Falafel Shop
11011 Brookhurst St, Garden Grove, CA 92840
Phone: (714) 603-7933

Hours

  • Monday - Saturday: 10:00 AM - 10:00 PM
  • Sunday: 10:00 AM - 8:00 PM

Open until 10:00 Monday through Saturday, and until 8:00 on Sunday.

What to Order

The Falafel Pita is the place to start — fresh-made, hot, and crispy, and it never falls apart. The Chicken Pita w/ Everything brings a spicy chicken with the perfect amount of heat. The Ribeye Hummus is the sleeper hit — steak hummus that regulars call unmatched. And the Sababa Fire Melt is the big one: a Jerusalem sesame baguette with prime ribeye, grilled onions and peppers, mayo, and melted American and provolone.

Why I Like It

It’s a young shop doing everything right: fresh falafel, generous pitas, and a menu that doesn’t overreach. A 4.8-star average across 2,000 reviews doesn’t happen by accident.

Best Time to Go

Lunch is the move — the falafel comes off the fryer hot and the pitas are at their best. Get there before the midday crowd if you can.

Final Thoughts

If you’re in Garden Grove and you haven’t had Sababa yet, you’re missing the best falafel in the county. Get the falafel pita and the ribeye hummus.
