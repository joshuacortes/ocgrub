Webb's Grainworks

A brewery, a distillery, and a restaurant under one roof — with a caboose for seating.

Overview

Webb’s Grainworks is the kind of place that makes you wonder why every town doesn’t have one: a brewery, a distillery, and a restaurant all under one roof. It’s family-owned — opened in 2023 by the grandson of auto dealer Lew Webb — and Orange Coast called it an O.C. unicorn, which feels about right. There’s a repurposed Santa Fe caboose for seating and a restored Ford Model T suspended overhead, and the whole room feels like it was built by people who love what they do.

The food is global comfort food with a smoker and a fryer doing the heavy lifting, and the beer and spirits are made right there on site.

Where to Find It

Webb’s Grainworks
7 Journey, Aliso Viejo, CA 92656

They don’t list a phone number and they don’t take reservations — just walk in.

Hours

  • Monday - Thursday: 11:00 AM - 10:00 PM
  • Friday - Saturday: 11:00 AM - 11:00 PM
  • Sunday: 11:00 AM - 10:00 PM

Friday and Saturday they stay open until 11:00, which makes it an easy late-week stop.

What to Order

Start with the Whipped Feta & Ricotta Dip — it’s the plate people photograph first. The House-Smoked BBQ Brisket Sandwich is the hearty move, and the Spicy Korean Wings come with sticky rice, pickled daikon, and buttermilk dressing. Don’t skip the Goat Cheese Wontons with raspberry sweet & sour either.

Why I Like It

It’s a brewery and a distillery and a restaurant, and all three parts are done properly instead of one carrying the other two. The room itself is half the fun — caboose, Model T, and a menu that doesn’t sit still.

Best Time to Go

Friday or Saturday evening, when the room is buzzing and the kitchen is going until 11:00. It’s the kind of place that works just as well for a casual dinner as it does for a night out with friends.

Final Thoughts

If you’re in Aliso Viejo and want something that isn’t another chain restaurant, Webb’s is the stop. Get the whipped feta dip, try whatever they brewed most recently, and take a look at the caboose before you leave.
