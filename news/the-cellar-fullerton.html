The Cellar Restaurant

French classics in a candlelit, cave-like dining room under downtown Fullerton since 1969.

Overview

The Cellar Restaurant has been Fullerton’s special-occasion French dining room since 1969, tucked into the basement of the historic Villa del Sol. The room itself is the story: a cave-like, chandelier-lit dining room built by craftsmen from Disneyland’s Pirates of the Caribbean. It’s Fullerton’s most storied date-night room, and a 2026 OC Register Readers’ Choice winner.

This is dinner only, opening at 4:00 every day, and it’s a splurge — the kind of place you book when the night matters.

Where to Find It

The Cellar Restaurant
305 N Harbor Blvd, Ste 001, Fullerton, CA 92832
Phone: (714) 525-5682

Hours

  • Monday - Thursday: 4:00 - 10:00 PM
  • Friday - Saturday: 4:00 - 11:00 PM
  • Sunday: 4:00 - 10:00 PM

Dinner only — the doors open at 4:00 every day. Reservations are recommended.

What to Order

Start with the Lobster Bisque, which regulars call the best they’ve ever had. The Classic Chateaubriand for Two is the signature occasion dish. The Filet Mignon — 10 oz Cape Grim with black truffle demi-glace and roasted garlic mashed potatoes — gets called the best filet some diners have ever had. Finish with the Sticky Toffee Pudding, lightly crispy outside and spongy inside, with vanilla ice cream.

Why I Like It

Rooms like this don’t get built anymore. A basement dining room from 1969, built by Disneyland craftsmen, still serving classic French cooking the long way. It’s a piece of Fullerton history that happens to serve an excellent dinner.

Best Time to Go

Friday or Saturday evening, when the room is at its best and the occasion feels earned. Book ahead — it’s a small room and it fills up.

Final Thoughts

If you’re celebrating in Fullerton, The Cellar is the room. Get the lobster bisque, split the Chateaubriand, and save room for the sticky toffee pudding.
