The Whole Enchilada in Placentia: Mexican Food, Margaritas & Family-Friendly Dining

Some meals are about trying something new, while others are about getting everyone around the table, ordering a favorite and enjoying time together. The Whole Enchilada in Placentia, California, offers a welcoming setting for both. With Mexican favorites ranging from tacos and burritos to combination plates, plus margaritas and a kid-friendly atmosphere, this roomy eatery is worth considering for a casual lunch, relaxed dinner or meal with the family.

A Roomy Mexican Eatery for Everyday Dining

Finding a restaurant that works for different appetites can make going out to eat much easier. The Whole Enchilada brings familiar Mexican fare into a spacious dining environment, making it a practical choice when you're meeting friends, bringing the kids along or simply looking for a comfortable place to enjoy a meal.

Rather than making the occasion complicated, the appeal is straightforward: sit down, look over the menu and choose something that sounds good. Whether you're in the mood for a filling burrito, tacos or a combination plate, the selection gives diners several classic options to consider.

A Place for the Whole Family

The Whole Enchilada offers a kids' menu and a roomy setting, two useful features for families planning a meal out. It provides an option for gatherings where adults and younger diners may have different tastes but still want to eat together.

Mexican Favorites, From Tacos to Combination Plates

Sometimes you know exactly what you're craving before you arrive. Other times, you want enough variety to weigh a few options. The Whole Enchilada features familiar Mexican dishes that make it easy to choose a meal based on your appetite and preferences.

Burritos for a Satisfying Meal

When a quick snack won't cut it, a burrito is a natural choice for a more substantial meal. It's a familiar Mexican staple that suits a casual lunch or dinner, particularly when you want something convenient and filling.

Tacos for a Classic Favorite

Combination Plates With Variety
