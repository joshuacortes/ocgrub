Cevapi: A Balkan Grilled Favorite

Cevapi is one of the best-known dishes associated with Balkan cooking. These small, grilled portions of seasoned meat are popular for their savory flavor and straightforward preparation. They're a natural choice for anyone interested in exploring traditional Bosnian food for the first time.

At Sofra Urbana, cevapi are part of the restaurant's focus on Balkan specialties. The dish can be a good starting point for guests who want to experience something rooted in regional food traditions rather than ordering a standard sandwich or salad.

If you're unfamiliar with cevapi, consider asking the team about the current preparation and how the dish is served. A quick conversation can help you choose a meal that suits your preferences.

Burek and the Appeal of Savory Pastries

Burek is another traditional Balkan food worth discovering. Made with thin layers of pastry and a savory filling, it has a place in everyday meals across the region and is often enjoyed as a filling bite for lunch or a casual meal.

Its pastry-based format offers a different experience from grilled dishes, giving diners another way to explore the variety of Bosnian and Balkan cuisine. For guests who enjoy baked savory foods, burek is a worthwhile dish to ask about when visiting Sofra Urbana.

Because fillings and serving options can vary, contact the restaurant for details about the current selection and ingredients before placing your order.
