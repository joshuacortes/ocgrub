Restaurant Spotlight · Huntington Beach, CA

Zubie's Dry Dock

Location 9059 Adams Ave
Huntington Beach, CA 92646
Phone (714) 916-5862
CALL RESTAURANT ↗

Sawdust Floors, Big Plates, and Forty Years of Surf City

Zubie's Dry Dock does not look like much from the parking lot, and that is the first thing I love about it. You walk in and the floor is covered in sawdust, the tables are long picnic benches you share with strangers, surfboards cover the walls, and there is a giant aquarium watching over the whole room. It is weird and wonderful and completely Huntington Beach, and the food is way better than a place like this has any right to serve.

I have been coming to Zubie's for years, and it never changes, which is the compliment. You order at the counter, you find a spot on a bench, and your food shows up in portions that make you glad you came hungry. It is the kind of place where a family with kids, a group of surfers, and a table of regulars watching the game are all having the exact same good time.

The room itself is half the experience. There is a 100-foot mural of local history running along the walls, a stone fireplace, arcade games for the kids, and TVs everywhere for the game. It feels less like a restaurant and more like a clubhouse that happens to serve really good seafood.

Overview

Zubie's Dry Dock has been a Huntington Beach tradition for more than forty years, in business at this spot on Adams Avenue since 1991, and it is still a single, independent location. The restaurant describes itself as a Surf City landmark, and after one visit you will understand why: the sawdust floors, the picnic-bench seating, the surfboards and the big aquarium are all part of a room that has barely changed while the city grew up around it.

It is a seafood restaurant and sports bar in the truest sense, with an oyster bar, a full bar, and a menu that runs from fresh fish to steaks to pizza. On review sites it sits around 4.4 to 4.5 stars with well over a thousand reviews, and I could not pin down a direct Yelp page link to share, so look it up by name if you want to read what diners are saying.

Where to Find It

Zubie's is at 9059 Adams Avenue, on the corner of Magnolia and Adams in Huntington Beach, with a big parking lot out front, which is a real blessing in this part of town. It is an easy drive from anywhere in HB, and there is room for large groups and team parties. Call (714) 916-5862 to place a pickup order or ask about a group visit. Note that some older listings show a different number, (714) 963-6362, but the restaurant's own website lists (714) 916-5862, and that is the number I used here.

Hours

  • Monday–Saturday: 11:30 AM–10:00 PM
  • Sunday: 9:00 AM–9:00 PM
  • Sunday Breakfast: 9:00 AM–12:00 PM

What to Order

The fish and chips in the photo is where I would start: big pieces of fried fish, fries, and a salad on the side, the kind of plate that needs two hands and a little room on the table. The oyster bar is the other signature here, and the Oysters Rockefeller are the order if you want to eat like a regular.

For something heartier, the 22oz rib eye and the prime rib are both legit, and the pizza is better than you would expect from a seafood joint. The wings are a good game-day call. On Sundays, come for breakfast: country-style breakfasts, omelets, and the famous breakfast burrito, served from 9:00 AM to noon. And if you are here on a weekday afternoon, happy hour runs Monday through Friday from 1:00 to 6:00 PM.

Why I Like It

I like Zubie's because it is the most Huntington Beach room in Huntington Beach. Sawdust on the floor, strangers sharing a picnic bench, a game on the TVs, and a plate of fish and chips the size of a hubcap. The portions are huge, the prices are fair, and nobody is pretending to be anything they are not. It is also one of the few places in town where a big group, kids included, can just show up and be comfortable.

Best Time to Go

Sunday breakfast is the move if you have never been: get there close to 9:00 AM, before the after-church crowd fills the benches. Weekday happy hour from 1:00 to 6:00 PM is the quietest time to eat and drink well for less. Game days are loud and fun, especially with NFL Sunday Ticket on, so come early if you want a good bench for kickoff.

Final Thoughts

Zubie's is a Surf City original, and it earns that title every day. Order the fish and chips, grab a bench, and stay a while. Places like this do not get built anymore, which is exactly why you should go while it is still pouring sawdust on the floor.
