OCGrub Deals Launch

Real discounts from independent OC restaurants — posted in our News section and updated every week.

Introducing OCGrub Deals

I started OCGrub because I kept hearing the same thing from people around Orange County: they love eating out, but everything keeps getting more expensive. So here's my answer to that — OCGrub Deals. It's a simple idea. Local, independent restaurants share a real discount or special, and I post it where you can actually find it. No app to download, no coupon code to hunt for, no signing up for anything. Just real savings at the places I already write about.

For now, you'll find the deals right here in our News section. That's where I'm posting them as they come in, and it's the first place to check. I'm updating the site every day and the deals get refreshed weekly — or any time a restaurant sends me something new, which honestly could be any given moment. If a spot drops a last-minute special on a Tuesday, I want it up on Tuesday, not next Monday.

What You Can Expect

Think happy hour specials, limited-time food deals, brunch specials, late-night deals, and the occasional "we made too much of this today, come get it" kind of offer. The exact mix will change week to week, because it depends on what restaurants actually send me — and that's the point. I'm not making deals up or scraping them from somewhere else. Every deal on here comes from the restaurant itself, so what you see is what's actually happening at that spot.

I'm starting with the places I already know and love across Anaheim, Irvine, Santa Ana, and Huntington Beach, and growing from there. If you've got a favorite local spot, check back often — the list is going to move fast, especially heading into Black Friday week.

Why It Matters

Look, eating out in OC isn't cheap anymore, and I don't think the answer is to stop going out. The answer is to make it easier to find the good spots when they're actually offering something. A happy hour that cuts your bill in half, a lunch special that makes a Tuesday feel like a treat — that stuff adds up, and it keeps money going to local owners instead of chains.

That's the part I care about most. Every deal on this site is from an independent, locally owned restaurant. When you use one, you're not just saving money, you're keeping a local spot busy on a slow night. That's a win for everyone.

For Restaurants: It's Free to Submit a Deal

If you run a restaurant in Orange County and you've got a special, a discount, or different hours for a holiday, I want to hear about it. Submitting a deal is free, and it always will be — no commissions, no fees, no catch. Just tell me what you're offering and I'll post it.

You can submit yours here: ocgrub.com/submit-deal. Send me the deal, the dates it runs, and the link you want people to use. I'll get it up in the News section as fast as I can, usually the same day. And if you've got something big coming for Black Friday, now's the time — I'm putting together our Black Friday guide right now and I want real deals from real local spots in it.

What's Coming Next

This is just the start. I'm working on the site every day, and the deals section is going to keep growing — more categories, more restaurants, and eventually a proper home of its own instead of living in the News section. Late-night deals, brunch specials, delivery discounts, all of it is on the way as more restaurants come on board.

For now, though, the deal is simple: check the News section, check it often, and come hungry. I'll keep it updated. You just have to show up.

Final Thoughts

I built OCGrub to help people find the best local food in Orange County, and Deals is the next step in that. Real savings, real local spots, updated all the time. Bookmark the News page, tell your friends, and if you own a restaurant — send me your deal. Let's make eating local a little easier on everyone's wallet.
