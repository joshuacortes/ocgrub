Restaurant Spotlight · Laguna Niguel, CA

Bottega Angelina

Location 32441 Golden Lantern
Laguna Niguel, CA 92677
Phone (949) 542-8220
CALL RESTAURANT ↗

An Italian Market Worth the Trip to Ocean Ranch

Bottega Angelina is not really a place I would send you for one perfect dish. I would send you for the whole experience: an Italian market, restaurant and bar in Ocean Ranch Village, run by Filippo and Shokoufeh Fusco, with a room that feels like a little trip to Italy and a patio worth lingering on.

Overview

Bottega Angelina opened in November 2021 in Ocean Ranch Village on Golden Lantern, and it is three things at once: an Italian market, a restaurant, and a bar. Owners Filippo and Shokoufeh Fusco built it as a woman-owned neighborhood spot, and the market side is a big part of the charm. You can browse Italian goods, sit down for lunch or dinner, and the whole place has a warm, old-world feel that stands out from the usual Laguna Niguel dining room.

Fair warning, and I want to be straight about this: at 3.8 stars across more than 950 Yelp reviews, it has the lowest rating of the five spots in this guide. People love the room and the setting more consistently than they love every plate. Go for the experience, the view, and a relaxed meal, and pick from the dishes the kitchen does well.

Where to Find It

Bottega Angelina is at 32441 Golden Lantern in Ocean Ranch Village, Laguna Niguel. There is plenty of village parking, and the location makes it an easy stop before or after a walk around the center. Call (949) 542-8220 ahead if you are planning a weekend brunch, since that is when it fills up.

Hours

  • Monday: Dinner only, 4 – 9 PM
  • Tuesday – Thursday: Lunch 11:30 AM – 3 PM, Dinner 4 – 9 PM
  • Friday: Lunch 11:30 AM – 3 PM, Dinner 4 – 10 PM
  • Saturday: Brunch 11:30 AM – 3:30 PM, Dinner 4 – 10 PM
  • Sunday: Brunch 11:30 AM – 3:30 PM, Dinner 4 – 9 PM
  • Closed 3 – 4 PM daily between lunch and dinner

What to Order

The lamb chops, served hanging on a rack with grilled bread, are the most memorable plate I saw come out of the kitchen, and they are a good shareable to start with. Beyond that, lean into the Italian basics the market side suggests: fresh pasta dishes, antipasti, and a glass of wine from the bar. Weekend brunch is a strong play here too, with the room at its liveliest.

Why I Like It

I like Bottega Angelina for what it is, not for what it is trying to be. The market, the bar, the Italian-goods shelves, the Fuscos running a family place in Ocean Ranch: it all adds up to a spot with real personality. Laguna Niguel has plenty of polished dining rooms. It does not have many places where you can buy imported pasta, have a glass of wine, and sit down to dinner in the same visit.

Best Time to Go

Weekend brunch, Saturday or Sunday from 11:30 to 3:30, is when the place is at its best. A weekday lunch is quieter and easier if you want to browse the market without a crowd. One quirk to plan around: the kitchen closes from 3 to 4 PM every day between lunch and dinner, and Monday is dinner only, from 4 to 9.

Final Thoughts

Come for the setting, the market, and a slow Italian meal, and order the lamb chops if they are on. Bottega Angelina is a Laguna Niguel experience pick, and on a sunny brunch morning, that is exactly what you want it to be.
