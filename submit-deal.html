Submit a Deal or Offer

Running a special? Tell Orange County diners about it. Deal submissions are free for locally owned restaurants.

What you can submit

Anything that gives a local diner a reason to come in this week.

  • Daily and weekly specials

    Taco Tuesday, happy hour, lunch combos and early-bird menus.

  • Coupons and discounts

    Percent off, buy-one-get-one, free appetizer with an entrée.

  • Group and party offers

    Catering packages, birthday perks and team-outing deals.

  • Limited-time events

    Grand openings, seasonal menus and holiday specials.

Include the start and end dates, any restrictions, and how diners redeem the offer. Only independently owned restaurants are eligible, and all submissions are reviewed before they are published.
