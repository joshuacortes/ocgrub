LJ's Lil' Cafe

From a shed in a Home Depot parking lot to Cypress's most talked-about breakfast burrito.

Overview

LJ’s Lil’ Cafe is the quintessential Cypress local-success story. John and Lydia Clarke opened it in September 2021 as a burrito shed in front of the Cypress Home Depot, and the OG breakfast burrito — tater tots, eggs, and a cheese pull that went viral — outgrew the shed. In October 2026 they moved into a bigger Cypress storefront, and the burritos moved with them.

Important: the old Home Depot location is closed. The current Cypress spot is the new Valley View location below — don’t go to the old address.

Where to Find It

LJ’s Lil’ Cafe
9515 Valley View St, Cypress, CA 90630
Phone: (657) 238-6110

Hours

  • Monday - Sunday: 6:30 AM - 2:00 PM

Every day, 6:30 AM to 2:00 PM. It’s a breakfast and lunch spot — get there early, because the burritos are the morning.

What to Order

The OG Breakfast Burrito is the one that built the place — Portuguese Hawaiian sausage, crispy tater tots, eggs, and the house signature sauce. The Chorizo Breakfast Burrito brings a house chorizo with a seasoning all its own, and the Spicy OG adds hot Italian sausage and jalapeños. The LJ’s Sammy is the breakfast sandwich version, and the Queso Tots are the shareable side. Everything comes with house-made salsas and sauces, including the creamy cilantro and habanero.

Why I Like It

It’s a family business that earned its bigger room one burrito at a time. The OG is still the best breakfast burrito conversation in Cypress, and the new spot means you don’t have to eat it in a parking lot anymore.

Best Time to Go

Early morning, right when they open at 6:30. The burritos are fresh, the room is calm, and you’re out the door before the day gets going.

Final Thoughts

If you’re in Cypress and you haven’t had the OG burrito yet, fix that this week. Go to the new Valley View spot, get the OG, and thank the shed later.
