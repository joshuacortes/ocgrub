A’Roma Ristorante

A family-run Italian dining room in La Palma with the classics done right.

Overview

La Palma doesn’t have a lot of sit-down Italian, which is exactly why A’Roma Ristorante stands out. It’s a family-run room — the Ferrari family has been behind it for years — the kind of old-school Italian place with a full bar, a happy hour, and a dining room that still believes dinner should feel like an occasion. Reservations are a good idea.

The menu sticks to the classics and does them well: handmade-style pastas, rich sauces, and the dishes regulars have been ordering for years.

Where to Find It

A’Roma Ristorante
30 Centerpointe Dr, Ste 1, La Palma, CA 90623
Phone: (714) 523-3729

Hours

  • Monday: Closed
  • Tuesday – Friday: 11:30 AM – 2:30 PM, 4:00 – 9:00 PM
  • Saturday: 4:00 – 9:00 PM
  • Sunday: 12:30 – 8:00 PM

They’re closed Mondays, and Tuesday through Friday they break between lunch and dinner — lunch runs 11:30 to 2:30, then dinner starts at 4:00. Saturday is dinner only.

What to Order

The Rigatoni alla Vodka is the signature — rich, smooth, and the plate the room is known for. The Fettuccine Alfredo is the other classic; regulars call it the best they’ve had, and it comes with grilled chicken if you want it. Start with the Calamari Fritti, crispy and straightforward. And if you’re in a seafood mood, the Linguine Frutti di Mare brings it all together.

Why I Like It

It’s the kind of place that’s getting harder to find: a family-run dining room, a full bar, a happy hour, and a menu that doesn’t chase trends. You sit down, you order the classics, and you have a proper dinner. La Palma is lucky to have it.

Best Time to Go

Weekday lunch is the easy move — quieter room, same kitchen, and you’re in and out in good shape. For a night out, Friday dinner is when the room feels most alive.

Final Thoughts

If you’re in La Palma and want a real Italian dinner without driving to a bigger city, A’Roma is the answer. Get the rigatoni alla vodka and settle in.
