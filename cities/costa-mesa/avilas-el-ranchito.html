Avila's El Ranchito in Costa Mesa: Homestyle Mexican Food, Cocktails, and Casual Dining

For diners looking for Mexican food in Costa Mesa with a family-owned feel, Avila's El Ranchito offers a casual setting centered on homestyle dishes and drinks. Located on Placentia Avenue, this restaurant is an option for everyday meals, relaxed get-togethers, and occasions when a private dining space or happy hour food fits the plan.

Avila's El Ranchito in Costa Mesa was a nice find when I was looking for a Mexican restaurant where I could enjoy a proper sit-down meal with a few different menu options. I wanted to try more than one dish, so I went with a combination of soup, a traditional Mexican plate, and fajitas. It was a good mix that allowed me to experience different parts of the menu, from something warm and comforting to a more substantial entree. The restaurant has the kind of atmosphere that works for an everyday lunch or dinner, but it also seems well suited for family outings and larger celebrations.

I began with Mamá Avila's Chicken Soup, which was priced at $16. This was a great way to start the meal, especially if you enjoy traditional Mexican soups. The flavors were bold without being overpowering, making it an enjoyable choice before moving on to the heavier entrees. I liked having something warm on the table, and it offered a different experience from the typical chips, salsa, and appetizer combination. If you are visiting Avila's El Ranchito and want something comforting, this is a dish I would recommend considering.

Next, I ordered the Carne Asada Plate for $23. The food was beautifully seasoned and cooked, and it was exactly the kind of traditional Mexican entree I was hoping to try. Carne asada is a classic choice for a reason, especially when you want a filling plate that brings together familiar flavors. I enjoyed this selection and thought it was a good representation of the type of meal many people look for when visiting a Mexican restaurant. It is also a solid option if you prefer ordering a complete entree rather than sharing several smaller dishes.

The Chicken Fajitas, priced at $22, were another selection I wanted to include. I always enjoy ordering fajitas when available because they bring a little more variety to the dining experience. They are also a convenient choice when everyone at the table has slightly different preferences, since you can put together each bite the way you like. Having the chicken fajitas alongside the Carne Asada Plate gave the meal two different protein options, while the chicken soup provided a lighter starting point. It was a nice combination for anyone who enjoys trying a few traditional Mexican favorites in one visit.

For anyone wondering what to order at Avila's El Ranchito in Costa Mesa, I would consider Mamá Avila's Chicken Soup, the Carne Asada Plate, and Chicken Fajitas. These were the three dishes I tried, with prices of $16, $23, and $22, respectively. They provide a good mix of traditional Mexican comfort food and familiar restaurant favorites. If you are dining with family or friends, ordering different entrees can also make the experience more enjoyable because everyone has the opportunity to try something beyond their own plate.

Parking is another practical consideration when visiting the Costa Mesa location. Avila's El Ranchito has parking available near its Placentia Avenue location, including spaces around the restaurant. For a regular lunch or dinner visit, having nearby parking makes getting in and out relatively convenient. However, the lot can become busier during popular dining periods, particularly when families and larger parties are arriving for dinner. If you are attending a scheduled group event or meeting several people, arriving a little earlier can help avoid unnecessary delays while looking for a space.

Overall, I enjoyed my visit to Avila's El Ranchito and liked having the opportunity to try several different dishes. Mamá Avila's Chicken Soup was a nice way to begin, the Carne Asada Plate delivered the traditional entree I was looking for, and the Chicken Fajitas added another familiar favorite to the meal. Beyond the food, the banquet facilities make this Costa Mesa restaurant a useful option for people planning larger celebrations or family events. Whether you are looking for a casual Mexican lunch, a full dinner with friends, or a venue that can accommodate a sizable gathering, Avila's El Ranchito is worth considering.
