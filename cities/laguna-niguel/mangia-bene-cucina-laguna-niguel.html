Restaurant Spotlight · Laguna Niguel, CA

Mangia Bene Cucina

Location 27281 La Paz Rd, Suite I
Laguna Niguel, CA 92677
Phone (949) 831-0141
CALL RESTAURANT ↗

A Laguna Niguel Italian Landmark Since 1991

Mangia Bene Cucina has been part of Laguna Niguel since 1991, and I finally understand why it has lasted. It is the kind of old-school Italian spot where the recipes outlast the owners: Reggie and Chris run it now, and they kept the dishes that made the place a landmark in the first place.

Overview

Tucked into the Plaza de la Paz shopping center on La Paz Road, Mangia Bene Cucina is a Laguna Niguel institution. It has been serving the neighborhood since 1991, and while the ownership has changed hands, the kitchen kept the recipes that built its reputation. Partners Reggie and Chris run it today, and the room still feels like the classic Italian-American restaurant it has always been: warm, unhurried, and built for regulars.

Yelp reviewers give it 3.9 stars across more than 950 reviews, while OpenTable diners rate it higher, around 4.6 to 4.7 stars. That split tells you something useful: the people who book a table and settle in for dinner tend to love it. This is a place that rewards sitting down, not rushing through.

Where to Find It

You will find Mangia Bene Cucina at 27281 La Paz Road, Suite I, in the Plaza de la Paz center in Laguna Niguel. There is shared plaza parking, and the restaurant is easy to spot once you are in the center. Call (949) 831-0141 for reservations, especially on a Friday or Saturday night.

Hours

  • Monday – Thursday: 11:30 AM – 9 PM
  • Friday – Saturday: 11:30 AM – 10 PM
  • Sunday: 11:30 AM – 9 PM

What to Order

The Rigatoni con Norcina is the signature, and it is the first thing I would point anyone to. The Cioppino is the other classic on the menu, a proper seafood stew for anyone who wants something beyond pasta. The Risotto Mare e Monti, with its land-and-sea combination, rounds out the trio of dishes the kitchen is known for, and the garlic cheese bread is the right way to start the table. Save room: the portions are generous in the way old-school Italian places tend to be.

Why I Like It

I like restaurants that know what they are and do not chase trends, and Mangia Bene is exactly that. Thirty-plus years in the same shopping center, same recipes, same idea of a good dinner. The new ownership could have changed everything and did not, which says a lot about how much the regulars matter here. It feels like a neighborhood restaurant in the best sense: the kind of place where dinner is the evening, not a stop on the way to one.

Best Time to Go

A weeknight dinner is the sweet spot, with an easy table and the full menu to work through. Friday and Saturday the kitchen stays open until 10, so it is a good pick for a later dinner, but those are also the nights to book ahead. Lunch runs every day from 11:30, which makes it a solid midday option too.

Final Thoughts

Order the Rigatoni con Norcina, get the garlic cheese bread for the table, and take your time. Mangia Bene Cucina has been doing this since 1991 for a reason, and one dinner there makes the reason clear.
