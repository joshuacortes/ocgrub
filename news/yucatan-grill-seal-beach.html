Yucatan Grill

Yucatecan cooking on Pacific Coast Highway — lobster enchiladas and cochinita pibil since 1988.

Overview

Yucatan Grill has been Seal Beach’s family-run Yucatecan spot since 1988 — thirty-eight years of lobster enchiladas, cochinita pibil, and jerk chicken on Pacific Coast Highway. Owners Felix and Georgiana run a single location, and it’s the only Yucatecan and Caribbean-leaning Mexican spot in town, which gives it an angle nobody else in Seal Beach has.

It’s a small, warm room with a menu that doesn’t look like the other Mexican spots on the coast.

Where to Find It

Yucatan Grill
550 Pacific Coast Hwy, Ste 111, Seal Beach, CA 90740
Phone: (562) 430-4422

Hours

  • Monday - Thursday: 11:00 AM - 8:00 PM
  • Friday: 11:00 AM - 9:00 PM
  • Saturday: 9:00 AM - 9:00 PM
  • Sunday: 9:00 AM - 8:00 PM

Saturday and Sunday they open at 9:00 in the morning. (Online ordering closes 30 minutes earlier than the dining room.)

What to Order

The Lobster Enchilada is the dish regulars keep ordering — it’s the plate people come back for. The Crab Enchilada is right there with it. The Cochinita Pibil is the must-order for anyone who knows Yucatecan cooking, though it’s worth asking if it’s on that day. And the Jerk Chicken with Beans and Rice brings the Caribbean side of the menu to the table.

Why I Like It

Thirty-eight years, one family, one location, and a cuisine almost nobody else in OC is doing. The enchiladas alone are worth the drive down PCH, and the cochinita pibil is the kind of dish that makes you glad you asked.

Best Time to Go

Saturday morning, right when they open at 9:00 — the coast is quiet, the room is calm, and the kitchen is just getting going. Lunch on the coast after works just as well.

Final Thoughts

If you’re in Seal Beach and want Mexican food that isn’t the usual, Yucatan Grill is the stop. Get the lobster enchilada and ask about the cochinita pibil.
