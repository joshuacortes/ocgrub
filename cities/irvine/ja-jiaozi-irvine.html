Restaurant Spotlight · Irvine, CA

JA Jiaozi Authentic Dumplings

Location 13776 Jamboree Rd
Irvine, CA 92602
Phone (714) 786-8999
CALL RESTAURANT ↗

Beijing Dumplings, One Irvine Shop

JA Jiaozi is the kind of place dumpling people in Irvine already know about. One shop, on Jamboree Road, making Beijing-style dumplings since June 2018 — and the rainbow steamed jiaozi, bright little parcels in a bamboo steamer, are the reason I tell people to go.

Overview

There is something I respect about a restaurant that has stayed in one place doing one thing for years. JA Jiaozi has been at it since June 2018 with a single Irvine location, no expansion, no gimmicks — just Beijing-style dumplings made the way they are supposed to be made. It holds about a 4.3-star rating on Google across roughly 580 reviews, and that tracks with my visit: a focused little shop that knows its craft.

The room is simple and the menu is focused, which is exactly what I want from a dumpling spot. You are here for the jiaozi, and everything else is secondary.

Where to Find It

JA Jiaozi is at 13776 Jamboree Rd, Irvine, CA 92602. Call them at (714) 786-8999.

Hours

  • Monday – Thursday: 11:30 AM – 8:30 PM
  • Friday: 11:30 AM – 9:00 PM
  • Saturday: 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM
  • Sunday: 11:00 AM – 8:30 PM

What to Order

Get the rainbow steamed jiaozi. They come in colored wrappers — yellow, green, red, black, and white — arranged in a bamboo steamer, and they taste as good as they look. Beyond those, work through the steamed and pan-fried dumplings; the Beijing style means thinner skins and generous fillings, and ordering a couple of varieties to share is the right way to do it. Keep it simple, order plenty, and do not skip the dipping sauce.

Why I Like It

I like that JA Jiaozi has not tried to become anything else. Eight years in one spot, one cuisine, done well — that is the kind of local business I want to keep around. The rainbow dumplings are fun, but the reason I would go back is the basics: good wrappers, good fillings, and a kitchen that clearly cares about the details.

Best Time to Go

Lunch is the sweet spot, especially on a weekday. Dinner gets busier, and the shop is small, so I would go early on a Friday or Saturday night. It opens at 11 on weekends, which makes it an easy late-morning stop too.

Final Thoughts

If you love dumplings and you have not been to JA Jiaozi yet, fix that. The rainbow steamed jiaozi are the photo, but the Beijing-style dumplings underneath are the reason to return.
