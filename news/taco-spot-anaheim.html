Taco Spot Anaheim

A late-night taco favorite in Anaheim known for bold flavors, fast service, and affordable street-style eats.

Overview

There's a new taco spot in Anaheim worth knowing about: Tacos Don Luna. It opened in early September 2026, and it's already the kind of place I'm sending people to when the late-night craving hits. It's a small, casual counter spot — nothing fancy, just good food made to order.

The menu leans into birria in a big way. Birria tacos with consomé for dipping, birria ramen, plus tortas, burritos, mulitas, quesadillas, and menudo on the weekends. It's the kind of menu where you order more than you planned to, and you don't regret it.

Where to Find It

Tacos Don Luna
2883 W Lincoln Ave, Anaheim, CA 92801
Phone: (714) 820-6100

Hours

  • Monday – Tuesday: 10:00 AM – 9:00 PM
  • Wednesday: Closed
  • Thursday: 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM
  • Friday – Saturday: 10:00 AM – 12:00 AM
  • Sunday: 9:00 AM – 12:00 AM

Heads up: they're closed Wednesdays, so plan around that. Friday and Saturday nights they're open until midnight, which is exactly when you want a place like this to be open.

What to Order

Start with the birria tacos — crispy, cheesy, and they come with consomé for dipping, which is the whole point. If you're hungry, the birria ramen is the move: noodles in rich broth with a bone-in piece of meat. And if it's a Sunday morning kind of day, the menudo is there for you.

There's also a salsa bar, so load up your tacos the way you like them — onion, cilantro, lime, and your pick of salsas.

Why I Like It

It's fast, it's affordable, and it doesn't try to be something it's not. You walk in, you order at the counter, your food comes out hot, and you leave happy. Anaheim has no shortage of taco spots, but a new one that stays open late and does birria this well is an easy add to the rotation.

Best Time to Go

Late evenings, especially Friday and Saturday. That's when the place earns its keep — most kitchens are closed, Don Luna is still going until midnight, and the birria is still coming off the line.

Final Thoughts

If you're in Anaheim and want straightforward, flavorful tacos without overthinking it, Tacos Don Luna is worth the stop. Come hungry, get the birria tacos, dip them in the consomé, and thank me later.
