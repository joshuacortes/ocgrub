Istanbul Grill California

Family-run Turkish cooking until 1 AM — mixed grills, Adana kebab, and serious hummus.

Overview

Istanbul Grill California is Fountain Valley’s family-run Turkish spot, and it’s the rare restaurant that’s still cooking at midnight. Family-owned and operated since 2013, it landed on Yelp’s Top 100 restaurants in the USA in both 2015 and 2016, and it’s TripAdvisor’s #1 restaurant in Fountain Valley. It’s halal, it’s open until 1 AM every night, and the kebabs are the real thing.

Late-night dining in OC is thin. This is one of the good answers.

Where to Find It

Istanbul Grill California
18010 Newhope St, Ste D, Fountain Valley, CA 92708
Phone: (714) 430-1434

Hours

  • Monday - Sunday: 11:00 AM - 1:00 AM

Open every day from 11:00 AM to 1:00 AM. (Yelp lists a 10:00 AM open; the restaurant’s own site says 11:00.)

What to Order

The Mixed Grill Kebab for two is the spread to get — a pile of kebabs that shows off the grill. The Lamb Shish Kebab is the one reviewers call the juiciest and most flavorful, and the Chicken Adana Kebab is a filling plate on its own. Start with the Appetizer Sampler: hummus, babaganoush, labneh with walnuts, and dolmas — the hummus alone gets called the creamiest around, and the babaganoush is the best some regulars have ever had.

Why I Like It

It’s a family spot doing one cuisine properly, late, every night. The grill work is serious, the appetizers are the kind you talk about after, and there aren’t many halal Turkish rooms in OC doing it at this level.

Best Time to Go

Late. That’s the whole point — when most kitchens are closed, Istanbul Grill is still going, and a mixed grill at midnight hits different.

Final Thoughts

If you’re hungry late in Fountain Valley, this is the answer. Get the mixed grill, start with the sampler, and don’t skip the hummus.
