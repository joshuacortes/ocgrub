Tacos: A Classic Part of Mexican Food Culture

Simple, Flavorful Combinations

Tacos are one of the most recognizable parts of Mexican cuisine, but their appeal comes from how much variety can fit into a simple format. A tortilla provides the base for a filling, while salsa, herbs, onions and other toppings can add freshness, spice and contrast. The combination can be straightforward or layered with several flavors, depending on the ingredients.

When you're in the mood for Mexican food in Garden Grove, tacos offer a familiar way to explore different seasonings and fillings without committing to one large entree. They're also a natural choice for diners who enjoy comparing flavors across a meal.

If you have questions about ingredients or heat, ask the restaurant before placing your order.

What Makes a Mexican Torta Different?

A torta is a Mexican sandwich typically made with a roll and filled with ingredients such as seasoned meat, cheese, avocado, beans, vegetables or sauces, depending on the style. The bread gives it a different texture from a taco, while the combination of fillings makes it a hearty option for lunch or dinner.

For anyone who wants something beyond tortillas, tortas offer another way to enjoy familiar Mexican ingredients. The sandwich format is convenient, filling and adaptable, making it an appealing part of a menu that includes both tacos and traditional entrees.
