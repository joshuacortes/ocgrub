Restaurant Spotlight · Huntington Beach, CA

The Longboard Restaurant & Pub

Location 217 Main St
Huntington Beach, CA 92648
Phone (714) 960-1896
CALL RESTAURANT ↗

Lobster Rolls and Game Day on Main Street

Every beach town has a bar like the Longboard. It is the one the locals actually go to, the one with the game on every screen, the one where the patio is full before the first pitch. I have been coming here for years, and it is still the easiest good time on Main Street: cold beer, a lobster roll, and a front-row seat to whatever Huntington Beach is doing that day.

The Longboard calls itself the original and oldest sports pub in downtown Huntington Beach, and it has the room to prove it. Surfboards on the walls, a bar shaped like a longboard, twenty-six TVs, and a front patio that might be the best people-watching seat in Surf City. It is loud, it is casual, and it is exactly what a Main Street pub should be.

What keeps me coming back is that the food is better than it has any right to be for a sports bar. This is not afterthought pub food. The lobster roll is the real thing, the clam chowder is proper New England style, and the menu has enough range that everybody at the table finds something they want.

Overview

The Longboard Restaurant & Pub has been on Main Street since 1991, which makes it about 35 years into its run, and it is still run by the same local family. It is a surf-oriented, New England-inspired restaurant and pub, and that combination is the whole idea: Maine lobster and clam chowder served a few steps from the sand in Surf City.

There is a second Longboard location on Springdale Street in Huntington Beach, also family owned, but the Main Street original is the one I keep coming back to. On Yelp the Main Street spot sits around 4.4 stars, and I could not pin down a direct Yelp page link to share, so look it up by name if you want to read the reviews. The regulars here do not need Yelp to tell them where to watch the game.

Where to Find It

The Longboard is at 217 Main Street, right on the main drag in downtown Huntington Beach, a couple of blocks from the pier. You cannot miss it: look for the crowd on the front patio. Street parking is metered, so give yourself a few extra minutes. Call (714) 960-1896 if you are bringing a big group.

Hours

  • Monday–Thursday: 11:30 AM–10:00 PM
  • Friday: 11:30 AM–1:00 AM
  • Saturday: 9:30 AM–1:00 AM
  • Sunday: 9:30 AM–10:00 PM

What to Order

Order the East Coast Lobster Roll, the plate in the photo, loaded with Maine lobster and served with fries and coleslaw. It is the signature here for a reason, and it is the thing I order almost every time. The New England clam chowder is the other must-have, thick and proper, and the fish and chips are a solid call if you want something fried and filling.

The Jamming Jerk Wings are the move if you are drinking and grazing, and the Baja chicken bowl is a lighter option that still eats like a meal. If you catch a Lobsterfest, the whole Maine lobsters are the event. Weekend brunch runs Friday through Sunday from 9:30 AM, and it is a much calmer way to do the Longboard than a Saturday night.

Why I Like It

I like the Longboard because it is honest about what it is and great at it. It is a surf pub with genuinely good seafood, cold beer, and a room full of people who are happy to be there. The front patio is my favorite seat in downtown Huntington Beach for watching the street go by, and the back patio is big enough for dogs and kids, which makes it one of the few Main Street spots that works for the whole crew.

Best Time to Go

Game day on the front patio is the classic Longboard experience, so come early if there is a big game on. For a quieter visit, weekday lunch is easy and the kitchen is quick. Friday and Saturday nights run late, until 1:00 AM, and the place turns into a proper pub after dark. If you want brunch, get there when they open at 9:30 AM on Saturday or Sunday and beat the crowd.

Final Thoughts

The Longboard has been doing this since 1991, and it shows in the best way. Grab the lobster roll, find a seat on the patio, and let Main Street do the entertaining. It is the most Huntington Beach afternoon you can have without getting in the water.
