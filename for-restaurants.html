How we pick

Every restaurant is independently owned (no chains, franchises, or corporate groups) and handpicked by a real person, not an algorithm.

Locally owned, always

No chains, franchises or corporate-owned groups. If a family or a local operator runs it, it's eligible.

Worth the drive

The food has to stand on its own. We look for consistency, care in the kitchen and something you'll want to tell a friend about.

Right for the occasion

A great taco counter and a great birthday venue are different things. We note seating, noise, parking and group capacity so you can plan with confidence.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything to be listed on OCGrub?

No. Listings and deal submissions are free for eligible restaurants. There are no commissions and no fees to appear on OCGrub.

Which restaurants are eligible?

OCGrub features independently owned restaurants in Orange County. We do not list chains, franchises, or corporate-owned groups. If a family or local operator runs it, it is eligible.

How do I get my restaurant featured?

Fill out the short application on our Get Featured page with details about your business. We review each submission and follow up about your listing.

Is every submission guaranteed a listing?

No. Every restaurant is reviewed and handpicked by a real person. Spots may be limited by city and category to keep listings high quality.

Can I submit deals, offers, or coupons?

Yes. Use our deal submission form to send in specials, coupons, and limited-time offers. Deal submissions are free.

Do I have to manage reviews on OCGrub?

No. OCGrub does not host customer reviews, so there is nothing for you to monitor or respond to.

What kind of businesses can apply?

Restaurants, cafes, juice bars, smoothie shops, meal prep services, and clean-eating concepts in Orange County can apply, as long as they are independently owned.

How does OCGrub help diners find my restaurant?

Diners browse OCGrub by occasion (group dining or everyday and date dining) and by city. Listings also give your restaurant more visibility in Google and AI searches.

How do I contact OCGrub with other questions?

Visit our Contact Us page and send us a message. We are happy to help.
