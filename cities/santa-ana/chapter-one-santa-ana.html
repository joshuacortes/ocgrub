Chapter One in Santa Ana: Modern Tavern Dining, Cocktails & Happy Hour

A great night out often starts with a place that feels different from the usual dinner spot. Chapter One in Santa Ana, California, brings a modern tavern atmosphere to a distinctive setting inside a former bookstore, pairing pub grub with cocktails and beer. With a fireplace, happy hour food and a private dining room, this lively destination offers plenty of reasons to make time for an evening out, whether you're meeting friends, planning a date night or organizing a gathering that deserves a little more personality.

For visitors who appreciate restaurants with a little individuality, Chapter One offers a different kind of night out in Santa Ana. Its blend of pub-style food and modern tavern energy creates an atmosphere suited to casual gatherings and social occasions.
