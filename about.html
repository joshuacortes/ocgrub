Our Story · Our Community · Our Table

Good food starts with knowing where to look.

OCGrub was created to make discovering great local food in Orange County easier. We're passionate about delicious food, thoughtful ingredients, and the independent restaurants that make our communities worth exploring.

Why OCGrub Exists

A better way to discover what's good around the corner.

Orange County is home to an incredible food scene. From neighborhood cafes and family-owned kitchens to restaurants serving fresh, locally sourced ingredients, there is always something new worth discovering.

We started OCGrub because finding those places shouldn't take hours of scrolling through search results, outdated listings, or endless reviews. We wanted to create a straightforward guide that helps people find restaurants based on what matters: the food, the ingredients, and the experience.

Whether you're looking for a healthier meal, a locally owned restaurant, a new date-night spot, or somewhere to bring the family, OCGrub is here to help you find your next favorite.

Meet the Founder

Built by someone who calls Orange County home.

Joshua Cortes · Founder

My name is Joshua Cortes, and I'm the founder of OCGrub. I was born and raised in Fullerton, California, and Orange County has always been home.

Growing up here gave me an appreciation for the different cities, neighborhoods, and communities that make up Orange County. Each has its own personality, local businesses, and food worth discovering. I wanted to build something that celebrates all of it.

My interest in food goes beyond simply finding a good meal. I'm especially interested in restaurants that prioritize quality ingredients, fresh preparation, healthier options, and local sourcing. I believe people should have an easier way to discover businesses that share those values.

OCGrub is my way of helping people explore the local food scene, support independent restaurant owners, and discover places they might not have found otherwise.

This is a locally rooted project, built with the goal of making Orange County's food community easier to explore and enjoy.

What We Believe

More than just another restaurant directory.

We believe finding a great restaurant should be about more than a rating or a sponsored placement. Our focus is on the food, the people behind it, and the experience of discovering somewhere new.

01 / LOCAL

Independent restaurants

We prioritize locally owned restaurants and the independent operators who give Orange County its character. Supporting local businesses helps keep our food communities interesting.

02 / INGREDIENTS

Quality over convenience

We're particularly interested in restaurants that emphasize fresh ingredients, thoughtful preparation, locally sourced products, and healthier food options.

03 / DISCOVERY

Places worth finding

From familiar neighborhood favorites to restaurants you haven't heard of yet, we want to make it easier to discover great food throughout Orange County.

Our Selection Process

How we choose restaurants.

Every restaurant featured on OCGrub should have a reason to be here. We look beyond a name on a map and consider several factors before highlighting a restaurant or recommending it to our readers.

Real-time customer feedback

We pay attention to current customer reviews and feedback to understand how restaurants are performing today. Recent experiences matter because a restaurant's quality and consistency can change over time.

Ingredient quality and sourcing

We look for restaurants that demonstrate a commitment to fresh, quality ingredients. Local sourcing, seasonal products, and thoughtful food preparation are all qualities that stand out.

Locally owned and operated

OCGrub is focused on independent restaurants, not national chains or corporate restaurant groups. We want to highlight the local businesses that contribute to our neighborhoods.

The overall dining experience

Great food is the starting point. We also consider the atmosphere, service, consistency, menu options, and whether a restaurant is worth recommending to someone exploring Orange County.

Local research and discovery

We research restaurants throughout Orange County, explore different cuisines and neighborhoods, and publish articles that help readers find food and dining experiences that match what they're looking for.

A guide that keeps evolving

The local food scene is always changing. We aim to keep our recommendations and restaurant information useful, relevant, and open to community contributions.
