Restaurant Spotlight · Huntington Beach, CA

Mangia Mangia

Location 16079 Goldenwest St
Huntington Beach, CA 92647
Phone (714) 841-8887
CALL RESTAURANT ↗

Sicilian Comfort Food, Huntington Beach Style

Mangia Mangia is the kind of restaurant you hear about from somebody's parents. It sits on Goldenwest Street, it has been there since 1987, and the people who eat there tend to come back for decades. I finally went to see what the fuss was about, and I left understanding it completely: this is real Sicilian cooking, served by the family that built the place, in a room that feels like somebody's dining room.

The first thing that hits you is how warm the place is, and I do not just mean the temperature. The brothers who own it are usually around, the staff treats you like a regular even on your first visit, and the complimentary garlic bread lands on the table before you have even decided what to order. That sets the tone for everything that follows.

It is not a big, flashy room. It is a cozy, family-run ristorante where the portions are generous, the sauces taste like they have been made the same way for forty years, and nobody rushes you out the door. That is exactly the kind of Italian restaurant I want in my neighborhood.

Overview

Mangia Mangia is owned and run by Sicilian brothers Giuseppe and Pietro Cefalu. After culinary school in Italy, where Pietro worked as head chef at some of the finest restaurants in the country, the brothers brought their cooking to America in the early 1980s and opened Mangia Mangia in 1987. Nearly forty years later, they are still the ones behind it.

The menu leans into their roots: authentic Sicilian dishes alongside recipes from other regions of Italy, made with fresh ingredients and served the way their family has always served them. On Yelp the restaurant holds a 4.2-star average across more than 1,000 reviews, and you can see them on Yelp. Regulars talk about it the way people talk about a family tradition, because for a lot of Huntington Beach families, that is what it is.

Where to Find It

You will find Mangia Mangia at 16079 Goldenwest Street in Huntington Beach, up in the north end of town. It is an easy stop if you are anywhere near Goldenwest or the 405, and there is parking right there. Call (714) 841-8887 to check on a table, especially on a Friday or Saturday night.

Hours

  • Monday–Wednesday: 4:00 PM–9:00 PM
  • Thursday–Friday: 11:00 AM–9:00 PM
  • Saturday: 3:00 PM–9:00 PM
  • Sunday: 3:00 PM–8:30 PM

What to Order

Start with the Meat Lasagna, the dish in the photo, baked until the cheese is bubbling and the meat sauce has soaked into every layer. It is the plate I would order if I could only order one thing here. The Calamari Fritti is the other must-order starter, crisp and straightforward, the way fried calamari should be.

From there, Scampi Cefalu is the house specialty, large shrimp sauteed with butter, garlic, white wine, fresh herbs, and a touch of cream. The Rigatoni Siciliana is eggplant, tomatoes, garlic, and basil, and it tastes like the island the brothers came from. The Linguine Frutti di Mare brings mussels, clams, calamari, shrimp, and fresh fish together in a light marinara, and the homemade gnocchi is worth asking about. Save room for the award-winning tiramisu or a Sicilian cannoli, and if they have the vanilla souffle, say yes.

Why I Like It

I like Mangia Mangia because it feels personal. You are eating food cooked by the family that owns the place, from recipes they grew up with, in a room where the owners know their regulars by name. The complimentary garlic bread, the generous portions, the fact that nothing on the menu is trying to be trendy, it all adds up to a restaurant that knows exactly what it is and has no interest in being anything else.

Best Time to Go

Keep the hours in mind, because this is mostly a dinner spot. Monday through Wednesday and Saturday, Mangia Mangia does not open until the afternoon, and lunch is only served Thursday and Friday. I like a Thursday or Friday lunch here, when the room is quiet and you can take your time, or an early dinner on a weeknight before the dining room fills up.

Final Thoughts

Mangia Mangia is the kind of place Huntington Beach is lucky to have: a family restaurant that has been doing one thing well for almost forty years. Go for the lasagna, stay for the tiramisu, and do not be surprised if you end up planning your next visit before you have even paid the check.
