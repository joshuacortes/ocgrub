Roma D'Italia

Tustin's first family-owned Italian restaurant, serving Old Town since 1961.

Overview

Roma D’Italia is Tustin’s first and original family-owned Italian restaurant, founded in 1961 by the Dominic Corea family and now in its third generation. Sixty-five years in, it’s still the Old Town room for handmade pasta, pepperoni bread, and tiramisu — with more than 2,100 Yelp reviews behind it and a dining room that feels like it’s been there forever, because it has.

It’s the kind of place where the menu doesn’t need to change, because it never needed to.

Where to Find It

Roma D’Italia
611 El Camino Real, Tustin, CA 92780
Phone: (714) 544-0273

Hours

  • Monday - Thursday: 11:00 AM - 9:00 PM
  • Friday - Saturday: 11:00 AM - 9:30 PM
  • Sunday: 11:00 AM - 9:00 PM

Open seven days a week, per the restaurant’s own site, with lunch served weekdays until 3:00 PM. (One Yelp listing snippet shows Monday closed — the restaurant’s own site says open daily.)

What to Order

The Gnocchi Catanzaro ($20.95) is the signature pasta — the plate the room is known for. The Spaghetti Sausage della Casa ($22.95) is the other classic. Start with the Pepperoni Bread ($15.95), and finish with the Housemade Tiramisu ($8.95).

Why I Like It

Sixty-five years, three generations, one room. Roma D’Italia is the rare restaurant that’s been doing the same thing well for longer than most of its customers have been alive, and the food still tastes like it matters.

Best Time to Go

Weekday lunch, before 3:00, is the quiet move — same kitchen, calmer room. Friday and Saturday dinner is when Old Town is at its best.

Final Thoughts

If you’re in Old Town Tustin and want Italian the way it’s been done since 1961, Roma D’Italia is the answer. Get the gnocchi, get the pepperoni bread, and save room for the tiramisu.
