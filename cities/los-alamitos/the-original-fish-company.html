The Original Fish Company: Seafood Dining in Los Alamitos

There is something special about a seafood restaurant that makes the ocean's flavors the centerpiece of the experience. A bowl of creamy clam chowder, fish cooked over a mesquite fire and the unmistakable aroma of freshly prepared seafood can turn an ordinary meal into something memorable. The Original Fish Company in Los Alamitos has built its reputation around these classic seafood pleasures, bringing together restaurant dining and a fish market for guests who appreciate seafood prepared with care.

A Longtime Seafood Favorite in Los Alamitos

Some restaurants become familiar names because they focus on what they do best. At The Original Fish Company, seafood takes center stage, giving diners a destination to consider when they're craving fish, shellfish and traditional coastal-inspired dishes. Its longtime presence and seafood-focused identity make it a recognizable choice for a sit-down meal in Los Alamitos.

Seafood has a character all its own. Depending on the preparation, a meal can feel light and fresh, rich and comforting, or smoky and savory. That range is part of the appeal, especially when a menu offers different ways to enjoy the catch of the day.

Whether you're planning a relaxed lunch, a dinner out or a gathering that calls for something a little different, The Original Fish Company provides a setting where seafood is more than an afterthought. It's the reason to visit.

Restaurant and Fish Market in One Location

The Original Fish Company is located at 11061 Los Alamitos Blvd, Los Alamitos, CA 90720. Along with its seafood restaurant, the business features a fish market. Call (562) 594-4553 for current dining hours, menu information or questions about the market.

Clam Chowder That Brings Comfort to the Table

Clam chowder has earned its place among the great seafood classics. Creamy, savory and full of familiar coastal flavor, it's the kind of dish that can stand on its own as a starter or help set the tone for a larger seafood meal. For many diners, a restaurant's chowder is one of the first things that comes to mind when they think about a satisfying visit.

The Original Fish Company is known for its clam chowder, making it a natural point of interest for anyone who appreciates traditional seafood fare. A warm bowl offers a different experience from a grilled or broiled entrée, bringing a rich, comforting element to a menu centered on fish and seafood.

A Classic Start to a Seafood Meal

Chowder can be a welcome choice before a seafood entrée, particularly when you're taking your time over lunch or dinner. Its creamy texture and savory flavor provide a contrast to the cleaner, more delicate taste of simply prepared fish.

If clam chowder is the reason for your visit, contact the restaurant to confirm current availability and serving options. Menu items and availability can vary.

Mesquite-Broiled Fish With Distinctive Flavor

One of the defining features of The Original Fish Company is its mesquite-broiled seafood. Cooking fish over mesquite introduces a smoky character that complements the natural flavor of the seafood without needing to overwhelm it. For guests who enjoy grilled or broiled preparations, that hint of fire and wood can be a major part of the appeal.

Fish is wonderfully versatile, but cooking it well requires attention to texture and timing. The goal is to bring out its natural qualities while creating a satisfying finish. Mesquite broiling adds another dimension, making this style especially appealing to diners who enjoy a savory, fire-kissed flavor.

For Guests Who Appreciate Seafood Prepared Simply

A good seafood entrée doesn't need to be complicated. The fish itself should remain the focus, with the cooking method contributing flavor and texture. Mesquite-broiled catches offer a classic option for those who prefer their seafood with a smoky finish rather than a heavy coating or elaborate preparation.

When ordering, ask about the current fish selections and preparation options. The available catch may change, so checking with the restaurant is the best way to learn what's being served during your visit.

Explore the Fish Market

The Original Fish Company offers more than a restaurant meal. Its fish market adds another dimension to the business, appealing to people who enjoy choosing seafood to prepare at home as well as those who prefer to have their meal cooked for them.

A seafood market can be a valuable resource when you're planning a home-cooked dinner. Selecting fish gives you the opportunity to decide how you'll season it, which cooking method to use and what sides will complete the meal. For anyone who enjoys experimenting in the kitchen, fresh seafood can open up plenty of possibilities.

Questions to Ask When Buying Seafood

  • Today's selection: Ask which types of fish and seafood are currently available.
  • Cooking recommendations: Find out which preparation methods work well for your chosen fish.
  • Portion planning: Ask for guidance on quantities based on the number of people you're serving.
  • Storage and handling: Confirm the best way to transport and refrigerate your purchase safely.

If you plan to shop at the fish market rather than dine in, call ahead to confirm its hours, available selections and purchasing details.

A Relaxed Way to Meet Up
