Hafez Cafe

A Persian Cafe That Starts With Breakfast

Hafez Cafe is the Persian breakfast spot in Laguna Hills — kaleh pacheh in the morning, complimentary chai on the table, and a cafe that feels like a neighborhood gathering place.

Laguna Hills has two Persian standouts on my list, and they do different jobs: Heaven on a Fork is my dinner kebab spot, and Hafez Cafe is where I go for breakfast. Hafez is the cafe — mornings, chai, and a table full of dishes you won't find at most breakfast spots in Orange County.

The cafe has been under new management since May 2026, and it's a single location with a loyal following: 4.6 stars across 335 Yelp reviews. The room is casual and welcoming, the kind of place where regulars settle in and stay a while.

Overview

Hafez Cafe sits at 24781 Alicia Parkway, Suite B — right next door to Bardega, in the same plaza — and it works as a Persian cafe in the truest sense: a place to start the day slowly, with real food and endless chai. The signature is kaleh pacheh, the traditional lamb head-and-feet stew that's a weekend-morning ritual for a lot of Persian families, and Hafez does it properly.

But it's not a breakfast-only spot. The menu runs through kebabs and tahchin too, so it works just as well for lunch or an early dinner. The complimentary chai is the touch that tells you what kind of place this is — they want you to stay.

Where to Find It

You'll find Hafez Cafe at 24781 Alicia Pkwy Ste B, Laguna Hills, CA 92653. Call (949) 600-9544 ahead if you're coming for kaleh pacheh, and see the Yelp page for what regulars are ordering.

Hours

  • Monday–Friday: 11 AM–9 PM
  • Saturday: 9 AM–9 PM
  • Sunday: 9 AM–8 PM

What to Order

If you come in the morning, the kaleh pacheh is the order — it's the signature, it's the dish people drive over for, and it comes with the kind of rich broth that makes you understand why it's a ritual. Later in the day, the kebabs are the reliable move, and the tahchin — that golden, saffron-crusted rice cake — is the side dish that steals the show. And the chai is complimentary, so say yes.

Why I Like It

I like Hafez Cafe because it's the morning half of Laguna Hills' Persian scene, and nobody else is doing this. A cafe that opens with kaleh pacheh and free chai is a place with confidence and hospitality in equal measure. It's unpretentious, it's generous, and it tastes like somebody's home cooking — which, in the best sense, it is.

Best Time to Go

Weekend mornings are the time to go — the cafe opens at 9 AM on Saturdays and Sundays, and that's when the kaleh pacheh crowd shows up. Weekdays open at 11 AM, so plan breakfast for the weekend and lunch or dinner during the week. Come hungry and unhurried; this is not a rush-through-it kind of meal.

Final Thoughts

Hafez Cafe is the breakfast counterpoint to Heaven on a Fork's dinner table, and Laguna Hills is lucky to have both. Come for the kaleh pacheh, stay for the chai, and leave with a new weekend-morning habit. Just remember the weekend opens at 9 — the early crowd knows what it's doing.
