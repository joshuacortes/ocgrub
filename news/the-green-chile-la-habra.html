The Green Chile

New Mexican cooking in La Habra — Navajo tacos, stuffed sopapillas, and Hatch green chile done the family way.

Overview

Some of the best meals in Orange County are the ones that taste like somewhere else entirely. The Green Chile is one of those places — a family-run New Mexican spot on Whittier Boulevard serving Hatch green chile, sopapillas, and Navajo tacos from recipes out of Tucumcari, New Mexico. The family opened their first spot in La Mirada in 2013 and brought the La Habra location to life in 2015, and it’s the only New Mexican restaurant of its kind around here.

This isn’t Tex-Mex and it isn’t Cal-Mex. It’s the real thing: green chile with actual heat, sopapilla dough made in-house, and plates that don’t look like anything else on the block.

Where to Find It

The Green Chile
2050 W Whittier Blvd, La Habra, CA 90631
Phone: (562) 448-3674

Hours

  • Monday – Thursday: 11:00 AM – 7:00 PM
  • Friday: 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM
  • Saturday: 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM
  • Sunday: 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM

Heads up: they close at 7:00 most nights, so this is an early-dinner kind of place, not a late-night stop. Saturday and Sunday they open at 10:00 in the morning.

What to Order

Start with the Navajo Taco — seasoned meat in fried sopapilla dough, topped with beans, cheese, lettuce, tomato, and chipotle ranch. It’s the dish people talk about, and it lives up to it. The Stuffed Sopapillas are the other must-order: homemade dough stuffed with beans, cheese, and your choice of meat, fried golden. If you’re in a burger mood, the Green Chile Cheeseburger puts the Hatch chile front and center. Save room for the Dulce de Leche Sopapillas at the end.

Why I Like It

There’s nothing else like it in the area. A family cooking their own New Mexico recipes, Hatch chile that actually tastes like Hatch chile, and a menu built around sopapillas instead of the same tacos you can get anywhere. It’s the kind of place that makes La Habra’s food scene more interesting just by existing.

Best Time to Go

Saturday late morning, right after they open at 10:00. You beat the lunch crowd and the kitchen is just getting going. Weekday lunch works too — it’s a quick, satisfying stop in the middle of the day.

Final Thoughts

If you’ve never had real New Mexican food, The Green Chile is the place to start. Order the Navajo taco, get the stuffed sopapillas, and don’t skip dessert.
