Simit: A Classic Turkish Bakery Favorite

Simit is one of Turkey's best-known baked foods. Often recognized by its round shape and sesame-coated crust, this bread has a satisfying contrast between its exterior and softer center. It's commonly enjoyed in the morning and pairs naturally with tea or other breakfast accompaniments.

At Canan's Kitchen Cafe & Bakery, fresh simit is a featured specialty. For guests familiar with Turkish food, it can bring a welcome taste of tradition. For first-time visitors, it's a simple introduction to a staple that has been part of everyday Turkish food culture for generations.

Simit can be enjoyed on its own or as part of a broader breakfast, depending on what's available. If you're curious about the best way to enjoy it at the cafe, ask the team about current offerings and suggested pairings.
