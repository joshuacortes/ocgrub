Mozambique

Peri-Peri Heat at Mozambique

Mozambique is not like the other restaurants on Coast Highway — and that is the point. South African flavors, peri-peri heat, and a dining room that stays open late make it one of the most distinctive tables in Laguna Beach. I went for the Mixed Grill and left already planning my next visit.

Overview

Mozambique is owned by Ivan Spiers of the ZA Restaurant Group, a privately owned local group with two open venues in Laguna — Mozambique and Rumari. It is a local operation, not a chain, and the South African roots run through the whole menu.

On Yelp it holds a 3.8-star rating across 2,732 reviews (Mozambique on Yelp). With that many reviews, the message is clear: people have strong opinions about this place, and the fans are loyal.

Where to Find It

You will find it at 1740 S Coast Hwy, Laguna Beach, CA 92651, on the south end of Coast Highway. Call (949) 715-7777 or visit mozambiqueoc.com for the current menu and reservations.

Hours

  • Monday–Tuesday: Closed
  • Wednesday–Thursday: 11 AM–10 PM
  • Friday: 11 AM–midnight
  • Saturday: 10 AM–midnight
  • Sunday: 10 AM–11 PM

What to Order

The Mixed Grill is the move — the plate I had came with a grilled steak, sausage, and peri-peri prawns, all in one spread. It is the best way to taste the kitchen in a single order, especially if it is your first visit.

If you like heat, the peri-peri prawns are the signature: plump, charred at the edges, and carrying that slow peri-peri burn. Order them as your main or add them to the table to share.

Why I Like It

I like that Mozambique gives Laguna something it does not get anywhere else. South African peri-peri is not a flavor you stumble across in Orange County every day, and the fact that it comes from a privately owned local group — not a concept shipped in from somewhere else — makes it feel like part of the town, not just a restaurant in it.

Best Time to Go

Come on a Wednesday or Thursday evening for the most relaxed experience, or lean into the late hours on Friday and Saturday — the kitchen runs until midnight both nights, which is rare in Laguna. Just remember it is closed Monday and Tuesday, so do not plan your visit then.

Final Thoughts

Mozambique is the boldest table on this list: peri-peri prawns, a Mixed Grill built for sharing, and hours that stretch past midnight on weekends. If you want a Laguna dinner that tastes like somewhere else entirely, this is it.
