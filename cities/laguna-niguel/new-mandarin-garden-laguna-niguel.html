Restaurant Spotlight · Laguna Niguel, CA

New Mandarin Garden

Location 23902 Aliso Creek Rd
Laguna Niguel, CA 92677
Phone (949) 495-0281
CALL RESTAURANT ↗

Family Chinese Comfort in Laguna Niguel

New Mandarin Garden is the kind of Chinese restaurant every neighborhood needs: family-owned, steady, and generous with the portions. Ginger and her husband Mike have run it since 2017, and between the indoor koi pond and a lunch special that runs about $14, it is one of the easiest family dinners in Laguna Niguel.

Overview

New Mandarin Garden sits on Aliso Creek Road, right next to Whole Foods, and it has been a Laguna Niguel standby for years. Ginger and her husband Mike are the current owners, running it as a family business since 2017. The dining room has an indoor koi pond, which tells you most of what you need to know about the vibe: it is a comfortable, family-friendly place, the kind where kids watch the fish while the food comes out.

It holds a 4.0-star rating across more than 450 Yelp reviews, and the lunch special, around $14, is one of the better midday deals in town. This is classic American-Chinese cooking done well, with a menu built for sharing and a room built for families.

Where to Find It

You will find New Mandarin Garden at 23902 Aliso Creek Road in Laguna Niguel, in the center next to Whole Foods, with shared plaza parking. Call (949) 495-0281 for takeout, which is a big part of what this place does.

Hours

  • Monday – Thursday: Lunch 11:30 AM – 3 PM, Dinner 4:30 – 9:30 PM
  • Friday – Saturday: Lunch 11:30 AM – 3 PM, Dinner 4:30 – 10 PM
  • Sunday: Lunch 11:30 AM – 3 PM, Dinner 4:30 – 9:30 PM

What to Order

The orange chicken is the signature, and the honey walnut shrimp is the other must-order. The sizzling black pepper filet mignon is the move if you want something a little more special, and the paper-wrapped chicken is the dish to try if you like ordering the thing nobody else at the table has had before. Come hungry and order family-style: the table fills up fast and the leftovers are the point.

Why I Like It

I like that New Mandarin Garden is exactly what it says it is. No reinvention, no fusion experiments, just a family running a Chinese restaurant the way they have been running it, with a koi pond in the dining room and a lunch special that is actually a deal. It is the kind of place you end up going back to because it never disappoints and never surprises you in a bad way.

Best Time to Go

Lunch is the best value, with the special running about $14 and the dining room at its most relaxed. Dinner is busier, especially Friday and Saturday when the kitchen stays open until 10. There is a mid-afternoon break between lunch and dinner, so plan around it: lunch ends at 3, and dinner starts at 4:30.

Final Thoughts

Order the orange chicken and the honey walnut shrimp, bring the family, and let the kids watch the koi pond. New Mandarin Garden is a Laguna Niguel staple for a reason, and it is the easiest kind of dinner to say yes to.
