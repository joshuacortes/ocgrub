Restaurant Spotlight · Laguna Hills, CA

Heaven on a Fork

Location 25461 Alicia Pkwy Unit A1
Laguna Hills, CA 92653
Phone (949) 382-2777
CALL RESTAURANT ↗

Persian Kebabs That Live Up to the Name

I keep coming back to Heaven on a Fork when I want a proper Persian dinner in Laguna Hills — smoky kebabs off the grill, saffron rice, and a dining room that feels like somebody actually cares.

Heaven on a Fork has been on my list since it opened in February 2022, and it earned its spot on Infatuation's best-of-OC list for 2026 the honest way: the food is just that good. One thing to know before you go — the restaurant moved to a new suite in the same plaza in mid-2026, so head to 25461 Alicia Parkway, Unit A1. Yelp still shows the old address, so don't let that throw you off.

This is a single-location, family-run kind of place, and it shows in the details. Nothing here feels like a chain version of Persian food. It's the real thing, cooked with care, and the room has that warm, low-key energy that makes you want to linger over the last of the rice.

Overview

Heaven on a Fork is a Persian and Mediterranean restaurant in Laguna Hills, and it's the dinner spot I think of first when kebabs are on my mind. The menu leans into the classics — grilled kebabs, stews, rice done right — and the kitchen clearly takes pride in getting the details right, from the char on the meat to the saffron in the rice.

It's a smaller, single-location restaurant, and the crowd reflects that: regulars, families, and people who drove over specifically for the food. On Yelp it holds a 4.4-star rating across 851 reviews, which lines up with what I've seen in the room — a lot of happy tables and a lot of cleaned plates.

Where to Find It

You'll find Heaven on a Fork at 25461 Alicia Pkwy Unit A1, Laguna Hills, CA 92653 — its new home in the same plaza it moved within in mid-2026. Call ahead at (949) 382-2777. It's also listed on Yelp, though the address there still shows the old suite, so use the new one above.

Hours

  • Monday–Thursday: 11 AM–8:30 PM
  • Friday–Saturday: 11 AM–9:45 PM
  • Sunday: 11 AM–8:45 PM

What to Order

Start with the beef koobideh — the photo says it all, two skewers of well-seasoned grilled beef with saffron rice, and it's the plate I'd order again tomorrow. The ali nazik is the other must-try: smoky eggplant and yogurt under grilled meat, rich without being heavy. If you're the kind of person who fights over the crispy rice at the bottom of the pot, order the tahdig trio and settle it properly. And the "heaven stew" — the house namesake stew — is the comfort-order move when you want something slower and deeper than a kebab plate.

Why I Like It

I like Heaven on a Fork because it doesn't try to be anything it isn't. It's a focused Persian kitchen doing its core dishes really well, in a room that feels personal rather than polished for Instagram. The kebabs come off the grill with real char, the rice is fragrant, and the tahdig has that crunch that tells you somebody in the kitchen is paying attention. It's the kind of place where dinner feels like an occasion without needing a special occasion.

Best Time to Go

Weekday lunch is the easy move if you want a quieter table — the room fills up on Friday and Saturday evenings. Dinner is when the place is at its best, though, with the grill going and the full menu in play. If you're coming on a weekend night, I'd aim a little earlier than peak dinner hour.

Final Thoughts

If you're looking for Persian food in South Orange County, Heaven on a Fork belongs at the top of your list. Go for the koobideh, stay for the tahdig, and remember the new address — 25461 Alicia Parkway, Unit A1. It's the dinner kebab spot in Laguna Hills, and it earns that title every time I go.
