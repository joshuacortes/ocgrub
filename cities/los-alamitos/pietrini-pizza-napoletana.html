What Makes Neapolitan-Style Pizza Special?

Pizza styles can vary considerably, and part of the fun is discovering which one suits your taste. Neapolitan-style pizza is associated with a tender, airy crust, a soft center and a balance of toppings that allows the dough to remain part of the experience. Rather than relying on an overloaded surface, the style puts emphasis on texture and the relationship between each ingredient.

That approach makes every component matter. The crust provides structure and character, the sauce adds brightness, and cheese and toppings contribute richness and flavor. When those elements come together well, even a simple combination can be deeply satisfying.
