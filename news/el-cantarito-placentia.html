El Cantarito

Family-run Mexican in Old Town Placentia since 2002 — chilaquiles, menudo, and Taco Tuesday.

Overview

El Cantarito is Old Town Placentia’s family-run Mexican kitchen, and it’s been doing it since 2002. Owner Francisco runs a small, bustling room where Taco Tuesday is almost a ritual, the menudo is precise and not heavy, and the tacos carry more flavor than they need to. It’s the kind of place where someone orders for the table and you just go along with it.

Dainty space, people waiting outside, indoor and outdoor seating — and a kitchen that doesn’t cut corners.

Where to Find It

El Cantarito
120 W Santa Fe Ave, Placentia, CA 92870
Phone: (714) 528-6090

Hours

  • Monday - Sunday: 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM

Open every day, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. (Some listings show 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM; the 8-to-8 schedule is the better-supported one.)

What to Order

The Chilaquiles con Pollo are the dish people call the best in town, red or green. The Chile Relleno is a menu staple and a customer favorite, and the Menudo on weekend mornings is the one reviewers call the best in all of Orange County. The Pozole rounds out the order, and the carne asada tacos are always a safe bet.

Why I Like It

It’s a family kitchen in the truest sense — generous portions, real Mexican cooking, and a room full of regulars. Placentia is lucky to have it, and Old Town is better for it.

Best Time to Go

Weekend morning, when the menudo is on and the room is at its liveliest. Or Tuesday, when Taco Tuesday turns the place into a ritual.

Final Thoughts

If you’re in Placentia and want real Mexican food without the fuss, El Cantarito is the stop. Get the chilaquiles, come back for the menudo.
