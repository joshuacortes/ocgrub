THE RANCH Restaurant

Dry-aged steaks, a wine list with ten straight years of awards, and a farm of its own.

Overview

THE RANCH Restaurant is Anaheim’s special-occasion steakhouse, and it earns the title. It’s a single-location, New American steakhouse in the MICHELIN Guide California, named Anaheim’s Restaurant of the Year and LA Times Best Steakhouse, with a Wine Spectator Best of Award of Excellence for ten consecutive years. And the kitchen cooks from its own organic heirloom farm up in the Santa Ana Mountains, which is the kind of detail you can taste.

This is a dinner-only room, open nightly from 5:00 to 10:00. It’s the place you book for a birthday, an anniversary, or the night you just want a proper steak done right.

Where to Find It

THE RANCH Restaurant
1025 E Ball Rd #101A, Anaheim, CA 92805
Phone: (714) 817-4200

Hours

  • Monday - Sunday: 5:00 - 10:00 PM

Dinner only, every night from 5:00 to 10:00. Reservations are a smart move.

What to Order

The headline is the 58 oz. Cowboy Ribeye, served with white-cheddar scalloped potatoes and bordelaise — it’s built for sharing, or for a very serious appetite. The 40 oz. Delmonico is 28-day dry-aged and bone-in, and the 50 oz. Porterhouse is the third member of the big-steak trio. If steak isn’t the call, the Jidori French Cut Chicken, finished with truffle butter, is the sleeper pick on the menu.

Why I Like It

It’s a steakhouse that controls its own supply chain, from the farm to the dry-aging room to the plate. The room is polished without being stiff, the wine list is genuinely deep, and the steaks are the real thing.

Best Time to Go

Any night works — it’s open nightly — but this is a place that rewards planning. Book ahead, come at 5:00 for a quieter start, and settle in.

Final Thoughts

If you’re celebrating something in Anaheim, this is where you go. Order the Cowboy ribeye, split it if you’re smart, and let the wine list do the rest.
