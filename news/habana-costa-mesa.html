Habana

Cuban classics and a candlelit patio at The LAB since 1995.

Overview

Habana has been Costa Mesa’s Cuban institution since September 1995, tucked into The LAB Anti-Mall with a candlelit patio and a live Latin vibe. Thirty years in, it’s still the room for ropa vieja, lechón asado, and paella — classic Cuban plates that no other OCGrub listing in Costa Mesa covers.

It’s part restaurant, part cocktail bar, and the patio at dusk is the whole point.

Where to Find It

Habana
2930 Bristol St, Ste A110, Costa Mesa, CA 92626
Phone: (714) 556-0176

Hours

  • Monday - Thursday: 11:30 AM - 10:00 PM
  • Friday: 11:30 AM - 12:00 AM
  • Saturday: 10:00 AM - 12:00 AM
  • Sunday: 10:00 AM - 10:00 PM

Friday and Saturday they stay open until midnight. The patio is best at dusk.

What to Order

Start with the Ropa Vieja — it’s the signature plate and the one the kitchen is known for. The Lechón Asado is the other classic, and the Paella is good any time. The Rum Pepper Shrimp is the dish reviewers keep coming back for, and if you’re still hungry, the Media Noche sandwich, the chicken empanadas, and the flan are all worth your time.

Why I Like It

Thirty years is a long time to do one thing well, and Habana has done it. The patio, the cocktails, the plates — it’s a complete night out in a part of Costa Mesa that does evenings right.

Best Time to Go

Friday or Saturday evening, when the patio is lit and the room is going until midnight. Get there before dark and watch the place fill up.

Final Thoughts

If you’re in Costa Mesa and want a night that feels like a night out, Habana is the call. Get the ropa vieja, sit on the patio, and order the flan.
